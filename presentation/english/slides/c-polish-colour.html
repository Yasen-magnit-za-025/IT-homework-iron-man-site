Motion polish

Posters wipe in, images zoom on hover, buttons get a sheen, and the Story section slides over Film like a sheet.

Colour

Section glows in gold and red, coloured outline words, and a filter chain that turns cast photos into a red duotone.

Reduced motion

If the visitor’s device asks for less motion, animations finish instantly and everything starts in its final place.

Part 19 (motion polish): poster and still images reveal with a clip-path wipe and settle from a slight zoom; images zoom gently on hover using the separate scale property, so it never clashes with other transforms; parallax layers get extra room; buttons get a sweeping light sheen and a gold focus ring; the nav indicator bar is styled; the Story section overlaps the Film section with a rounded top and shadow while Film dims behind it (--cover); a glint runs down the timeline; and two faint HUD rings float in the Tech background. Part 20 (colour): radial-gradient glows for Story, Armory, Cast and Trilogy; gold and red outlines for heading words; tinted badges; the red duotone for inactive cast photos, made by chaining grayscale, sepia, hue-rotate and saturate filters; richer story images. The final block is prefers-reduced-motion: it makes every animation and transition finish instantly, cancels the scroll scenes, shows all hidden text and hides the purely decorative effects.
