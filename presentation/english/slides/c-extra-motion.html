Custom cursor

A glowing reactor ring follows the mouse and turns red over anything clickable; clicks fire a shockwave.

Letters from data

While a letter scrambles, ::before shows its data-g attribute (a random glyph) in cyan instead of the real letter.

Printed paragraphs

A paragraph starts clipped to nothing and is revealed left to right while a glowing bar runs along the edge.

Part 17 holds the extra motion. The .cursor is a fixed ring with a dashed inner ring and a dot; is-hover makes it bigger and red. .shock is the expanding ring that appears on each click. The hero embers canvas sits over the background with screen blending. A one-time cyan scan line sweeps down the hero when it powers on, and the title flickers on with a stepped animation. Armor cards get a glare that follows the mouse, and the ticker words lean with --skew. Part 18 is the HUD text. Each heading letter is a .hud-ch span with a data-s state: "off" is invisible, "scan" hides the real letter and shows a random cyan glyph from data-g through ::before, and "lock" plays a quick glow flash. Paragraphs with .hud-print start fully clipped; .is-printed animates the clip open and moves a glowing scan bar across in sync, while the text cools from cyan.
