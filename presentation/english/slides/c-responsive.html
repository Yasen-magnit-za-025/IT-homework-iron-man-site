Laptop: 1180px

Three‑column layouts drop to two, so text columns never get too narrow to read.

Tablet: 960px

The menu becomes a full‑screen overlay behind the hamburger; side‑by‑side sections stack.

Phone: 720px

Smaller type and chips, shorter scroll scenes, re‑aimed images, and the schematic’s callouts pulled inward.

Part 16 is three media queries that adjust the layout as the screen gets narrower. Under 1180px: the nav links get tighter, the film grid goes to two columns with the facts below, and tech panels stack. Under 960px: the hamburger appears and the links become a full-screen overlay where each link slides in one after another; the notes, stats, timeline, cast, villains, trilogy, footer and dossier all switch to stacked layouts; the timeline rail moves to the left edge. Under 720px: the hero is shorter (220vh) with no cast names, a smaller reactor and a stacked footer; the interlude is 200vh and re-aimed at the firing palm; armory cards get phone widths; movie posters stack; and the tech callouts move to a 38% radius with smaller text so all seven fit. There is also a short-screen rule that hides the hero intro on laptops under 760px tall, and a hover:none rule for touch screens.
