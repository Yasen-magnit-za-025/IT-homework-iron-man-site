Stacked layers

Suit and thrusters

The two thruster spans live inside the suit’s wrapper, so the flames move with him when he lifts off.

Lines 57 to 135 build the opening screen. The section has id "top" so the brand link can scroll back to it. hero__pin is the frame that stays fixed while you scroll (CSS makes it sticky). Inside it: hero__bg holds the darkened workshop photo; hero__scrim is a gradient that keeps text readable; the canvas is where JavaScript draws the rising embers. hero__stage holds four layers that move at different speeds: the reactor ring, the ground glow, the title (cast names, the h1 "Iron Man" split into two words, and the vertical [2008]) and the suit. hero__suit wraps the Mark III image and the two thruster spans, positioned under each boot. Below the stage, hero__foot holds the tagline, a short intro, three round fact chips and the "Explore the universe" link. Elements with data-in fade in one after another when the page is ready, using the --i number on each.
