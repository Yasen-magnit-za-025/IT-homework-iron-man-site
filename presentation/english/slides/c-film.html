Grid layouts

Poster, story and facts share one CSS grid with fixed proportions; the stats use a four‑column grid.

Part 6 has the ticker: a red band rotated slightly, its track a flex row of words moved left by a 40-second linear marquee animation. The words are separated by small dots made with ::after. The Film section uses the paper colour and dark ink, with a light graph-paper texture made from two repeating linear gradients and a faint red glow. .film__title is the huge "Directed by Jon Favreau" text with the second line pushed right. .film__grid is a three-column grid for poster, copy and facts. The poster gets a red corner bracket using ::before. The cast and facts lists use borders between rows. .stats is a four-column grid of big numbers, and .notes styles the accordion: the plus icon is two lines, and .is-open rotates it and expands the answer using grid-template-rows from 0fr to 1fr, which animates height smoothly.
