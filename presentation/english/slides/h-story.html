The blast interlude

A pinned frame holds the photo, three glow spans on the palms and chest, a HUD lock‑on and a flash layer.

The Story section (id "story") has a heading and an ordered list. The first li is the rail, a vertical line that fills as you scroll. The other six li elements are the chapters: a figure with a film still, a node dot on the rail, and the text block with a big outlined number, the place, an h3 title and a short paragraph. Every image has alt text describing what it shows. Lines about 318 to 343 are the interlude section ("band"): it is taller than the screen with a pinned frame inside. band__frame keeps the image at its own shape so the three "charge" glows can be placed exactly on the palms and chest with percentages. band__hud has the lock-on ring and the charge readout, band__flash is the white-out and shockwave rings, and band__copy holds the "Built in a cave / Perfected in a workshop" text.
