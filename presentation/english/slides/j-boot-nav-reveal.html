Nav, counters, notes

The nav hides on scroll down and tracks the current section; counters ease up to their number; one note opens at a time.

initBoot waits for the hero images and fonts with Promise.race against a timeout, removes is-booting (hiding the boot screen) and adds is-ready to the hero. initNav opens and closes the phone menu (with Esc and on resize too), uses an IntersectionObserver as a scroll-spy to mark the current section’s link, and returns an update function that adds is-scrolled, hides the nav while scrolling down, and sets --p for the progress bar. initReveal staggers groups with --d, then watches every data-reveal element (wipes through their parent, because a fully clipped element never reports as visible) and adds is-in once. initCounters counts each data-count number up over 1.7 seconds with an ease-out curve when 60% of it is on screen. initNotes makes the accordion open one note at a time and keeps aria-expanded accurate.
