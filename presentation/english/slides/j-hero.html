Turning scroll position into a launch

const phase = (p, a, b) => {
  const t = clamp((p - a) / (b - a), 0, 1);
  return t * t * (3 - 2 * t);

Progress p

How far you’ve scrolled through the pinned hero, from 0 at the top to 1 when it lets go.

phase() windows

phase turns a slice of p into a smooth 0→1: ignition runs from 4% to 28%, lift‑off from 20% to 95%.

Driving the scene

Those values set the suit’s position, the --flame size, the ground glow, the title split and a camera shake.

initHero has two jobs. First, pointer depth: on mouse move it sets a target from −1 to 1 and eases --mx and --my toward it each frame, so the hero layers drift. Second, the scroll take-off. travel is how far you can scroll while the hero is pinned (section height minus one screen), and p is your progress from 0 to 1. phase(p, a, b) maps the window a to b onto 0 to 1 with a smoothstep curve, so effects start and finish gently. ignite drives the thrusters and ground blast. rise drives the lift-off, and lift = rise to the power 2.2 makes it start slowly then rocket. The suit crouches a little while igniting, then moves up by lift times 1.3 screens, grows and tilts. The title words slide apart by split × 34vw and fade. Cast names, year and bottom copy fade first. The reactor ring swells. A random shake is applied while igniting. heroThrust tells the embers to speed up.
